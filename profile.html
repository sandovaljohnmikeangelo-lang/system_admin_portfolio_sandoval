Career Progression

System Administrator Pathway

Build practical experience step by step and advance toward infrastructure leadership.

01
Entry Level

IT Support Technician

Provides first-line technical assistance and resolves basic issues for users and devices.

  • Triage service tickets and remote calls
  • Configure workstations and peripherals
  • Reset accounts and basic permissions
  • Document solutions and escalation
02
Intermediate

Junior System Administrator

Maintains servers, operating systems, user access, updates, and routine infrastructure tasks.

  • Manage accounts and permissions
  • Monitor health and apply updates
  • Perform backups and recovery tests
  • Automate routine administrative work
03
Advanced

Senior System Administrator

Owns complex infrastructure, leads projects, improves security, and supports team development.

  • Design resilient infrastructure
  • Plan migrations and capacity
  • Strengthen security and compliance
  • Mentor junior technical staff

Team Positions

Job Roles & Responsibilities

Related roles work at different levels of the technology environment.

01

Help Desk Technician

Primary focus: users. Receives and resolves support requests, diagnoses common hardware or software problems, and escalates issues requiring deeper access.

02

System Administrator

Primary focus: infrastructure. Maintains servers, networks, accounts, backups, and security controls while ensuring applications remain available and performant.

03

Systems Engineer

Primary focus: architecture. Designs and improves enterprise systems, automates deployment, evaluates platforms, and leads complex technology projects.
